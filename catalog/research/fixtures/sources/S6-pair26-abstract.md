# PAIR-26: a randomized trial of AI pair programming (synthetic fixture abstract)

Design: PAIR-26 was a randomized controlled trial in 240 professional software developers at three companies.

Intervention: an AI pair-programming assistant; comparator: no assistant.

Selection: allocation was concealed through central web-based randomization.

Performance: participants and their team leads were not blinded to group assignment.

Detection: outcome assessors who scored the code reviews were blinded to group assignment.

Attrition: 29 of 240 participants (12%) left before the final assessment, balanced across groups.

Reporting: the primary outcome was pre-registered and all registered outcomes are reported.

Result: median task completion time was 18% shorter with the assistant.
